## Supply Policy
PAPA is designed around a fixed maximum supply of 10,000,000 PAPA.

The intended Mainnet launch process is:
1. Create the final Mainnet mint.
2. Mint exactly 10,000,000 PAPA.
3. Distribute the supply according to the allocations above.
4. Configure and verify the required vesting positions.
5. Verify metadata, balances and token accounts.
6. Revoke mint authority only after all launch checks are complete.

No freeze authority is intended for the final token.
